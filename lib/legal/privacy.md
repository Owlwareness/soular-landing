# Privacy Policy

Caive LLC · Soular
Version 2.0 · Effective October 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Caive LLC ("Caive", "we", "us" or "our") collects, uses, shares and protects personal information when you use the Soular mobile application (listed on the App Store as SoularLove) and related services (together, the "Service"). By using the Service, you acknowledge that you have read this Privacy Policy.

## 1. Information We Collect

**1.1 Information you provide**

- **Account information:** your email address, password, first name and date of birth. Passwords are stored by our authentication provider in hashed form and are not visible to us.
- **Profile photo:** the photo you upload to your profile.
- **Content:**
  - messages you send to your matches
  - responses you write in Star Forge
  - Glimpse notes you choose to share
  - any details you include in a report
- **Communications:** information you provide when you contact us.

**1.2 Information generated by your use of the Service**

- **Matches:** the people you have been matched with, and when.
- **Shared maps:** the stars and links generated from your conversations with each match, and whether photos have been unlocked for a match.
- **Personal maps:** prompts derived from your conversations and the personal stars you create from them.
- **Safety records:** blocks and reports, including any messages attached to a report (see Section 6).
- **Consent records:** the version and date of the Terms of Service, this Privacy Policy and the AI processing disclosure you accepted.

**1.3 Information collected automatically**

- **Device push token:** if you enable notifications, used to deliver them through Apple Push Notification service (iOS) or Google Firebase Cloud Messaging (Android).
- **Technical information:** such as IP address, request times and error logs, recorded by our service providers when you use the Service. This information is retained for a limited period for security and reliability purposes.

We do not collect precise location, contacts or advertising identifiers, and the app does not contain third-party analytics or advertising tools.

## 2. How We Use Information

We use personal information to:
- provide and operate the Service, including daily matching, messaging, shared and personal maps, photo unlocking and Glimpses
- process conversations with artificial intelligence, as described in Section 4
- send notifications you have enabled, such as new matches, messages, stars and photo unlocks
- verify that users meet our minimum age requirement
- review reports, enforce our Terms of Service and protect the safety of our users
- maintain the security and integrity of the Service
- comply with legal obligations

We do not sell personal information, use it for targeted advertising or allow it to be used to train AI models.

**Legal bases (UK and EEA).** We process personal information to perform our contract with you (including AI processing, which is part of the Service), on the basis of your consent (requested before any conversation is processed with AI), for our legitimate interests in keeping the Service safe and secure, and to comply with legal obligations.

## 3. How We Share Information

**3.1 With your matches**

Your matches can see your first name, the messages you send them, your shared map with them, and any Glimpse you choose to share. Your profile photo is shown to a match only after photos are unlocked for that match (after the second AI mapping, at four stars).

**3.2 With service providers**

We share information with service providers that process it on our behalf and under our instructions:

- **Supabase (United States):** database, authentication and file storage. Processes account information, content, maps and profile photos.
- **OpenRouter and its zero-data-retention AI hosting provider (currently Microsoft Azure):** AI processing for mapping and Star Forge. Processes conversation text as described in Section 4.
- **Expo, Apple Push Notification service and Google Firebase Cloud Messaging:** delivery of notifications. Processes push tokens and notification text.
- **Resend:** email delivery. Processes email addresses for account emails, and report details sent to our team.
- **Vercel:** hosting of the soular.love website. Processes standard website request logs.

**3.3 For legal reasons**

We may disclose information if required by law, or where we believe disclosure is necessary to protect the rights, property or safety of any person.

**3.4 Business transfers**

If Caive is involved in a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, personal information may be transferred as part of that transaction, subject to this Privacy Policy.

## 4. Artificial Intelligence Processing

**4.1 Purpose**

The Service uses an AI model to:
1. assess whether both people in a conversation have shared something meaningful
2. generate stars and links for your shared map, and prompts for each person's personal map
3. generate personal stars from your Star Forge responses

**4.2 Information sent**

For a conversation, the text sent since the last mapping and the titles of existing stars on the shared map. For Star Forge, the conversation excerpt the prompt came from, your written response and your existing personal stars.

**4.3 Information not sent**

Your email address, profile photo, date of birth and account identifiers are never included in AI requests. Before a request is sent, both participants' profile names are replaced with placeholders, and they are restored on our servers afterwards. A profile name that is also a common word is replaced only when it is capitalized. Any other information you type, such as another person's name, is sent as written.

**4.4 AI provider**

Requests are routed by OpenRouter to OpenAI's GPT-6 Luna model, hosted by a provider that has agreed to zero data retention, meaning it does not store or train on the data. That provider is currently Microsoft Azure. Every request requires zero data retention; if no compliant provider is available, the request fails. OpenRouter may screen content for illegal material, and flagged content may be retained under OpenRouter's policies for safety or legal reasons.

**4.5 Consent and withdrawal**

All users consent to AI processing during sign-up, before any conversation is processed. Because AI processing is an essential part of the Service, consent can be withdrawn by deleting your account in Settings. No further information is sent after deletion.

**4.6 Accuracy**

AI-generated content reflects an automated interpretation of a conversation. It may be inaccurate and is not professional advice of any kind.

## 5. Profile Photos

Your profile photo is displayed to a match only after photos are unlocked for that match. Photo files are currently stored at addresses that are not publicly listed but could be opened by anyone who obtains the exact address. We are moving photo storage to private access controls and will update this Privacy Policy when that work is complete.

## 6. Data Retention

- **Active accounts:** we retain personal information while your account is active and as needed to provide the Service.
- **Blocks:** blocking a user ends the match and deletes the messages between you.
- **Reports:** reporting a user also blocks them. A copy of the most recent 50 messages in the conversation is retained for review until either account is deleted.
- **Account deletion:** deleting your account immediately deletes your profile, profile photo, messages, matches, shared and personal maps, Glimpses, blocks and reports. If a file cannot be deleted immediately, deletion is retried until it succeeds.
- **Logs and backups:** service provider logs and backups expire automatically according to their retention schedules.

## 7. Your Rights and Choices

- **Access and portability:** you may request a copy of your personal information.
- **Correction:** you may update your profile in the app or contact us.
- **Deletion:** you may delete your account at any time in Settings, or request deletion without the app at https://soular.love/delete.
- **AI processing:** see Section 4.5.
- **Notifications:** you may disable notifications in your device settings.
- **UK and EEA residents** may also object to or request restriction of processing, and may lodge a complaint with their local data protection authority.

We respond to requests within 30 days and may verify that a request comes from the email address associated with the account.

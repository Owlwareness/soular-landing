# Delete your SoularLove account

Caive LLC · Soular

**In the app**

Open SoularLove, go to Settings and tap Delete Account. Your account is deleted immediately.

**Without the app**

If you no longer have the app, email hello@soular.love from the email address on your account, with the subject "Delete my account". We will confirm the request comes from that address and delete the account within 30 days, usually much sooner.

**What is deleted**

- your profile and profile photo
- your messages and matches
- your shared and personal maps
- your Glimpses
- your blocks and reports

**What may remain**

Our service providers' logs and backups expire automatically on their own retention schedules. See our [Privacy Policy](/privacy) for details.
